1. Booking and payment

1.1 Payment options

Every project is priced by a written proposal I send you through your private project page. The proposal sets the price, delivery time, revisions and deposit for that project. When you accept it, you pay one of two ways:

  • 50% deposit. Half of the quoted total is paid up front to reserve studio time. The remaining balance is due once you approve the final mix, and before the uncompressed master and stems are released.
  • Pay in full. The full quoted total is paid up front. Deliverables release as soon as you approve the final version.

All payments are processed by Stripe. I never see or store your card details. Prices are quoted in US dollars.

1.2 When payments are due

Payment is made in up to two stages. The deposit reserves your booking and covers session preparation. The balance is due when you approve the final mix. Approval happens when you tell me in writing that the mix is final, or automatically fourteen days after I deliver a version and receive no further notes.

Final uncompressed masters, multitrack mix stems and alternate passes are released only after the full balance has cleared. Streaming references at lower quality are shared throughout the project so you can review.

1.3 Cancellations and refunds

  • Cancel before I open your session and you get a full refund of anything paid, minus Stripe's processing fee.
  • Once I have opened your session and started work, the deposit is non refundable because that time has been committed to your project.
  • If I can't complete the work for reasons on my side, you get a full refund of anything paid for the unfinished part of the project.

2. Deliverables and revisions

Every mixing or mastering project includes, at no extra cost:

  • Full Mix / Master
  • Instrumental Pass
  • Acapella Pass
  • TV Track
  • Multitrack Mix Stems

Each song includes two rounds of revisions. Please send notes for each round together in one message. Further rounds, new vocal takes, arrangement changes or replacement stems sent after mixing has started are billed at [$RATE / hour] or a flat fee we agree in writing first.

2.1 Live drum recording

Live Drum Recording is an optional add on billed per session at [$RATE / session]. A session covers setup, tracking and a comp of the takes. Scheduling is confirmed by email after booking.

2.2 Timing

Turnaround starts once usable stems are received and the deposit or full payment has cleared. I'll confirm a delivery date by email. Delays caused by missing or unusable files pause the schedule.

3. Stem delivery requirements

Upload your files to the private Dropbox folder linked in your confirmation. Please export:

  • 24-bit WAV at the session's native sample rate (44.1 kHz or higher). No MP3 or other lossy files.
  • Every stem consolidated from bar 1 beat 1, so all files line up when imported.
  • With master bus limiters, clippers and bus compression switched off.
  • With clear names, and the tempo and key in the folder name.
  • With a rough mix or reference bounce included.

If files arrive clipped, misaligned or incomplete, I'll ask for new exports before starting. Time spent fixing unusable files may be billed at the hourly rate above, with your approval first.

4. Rights and credit

You keep ownership of your songs and recordings. I make no claim to publishing, songwriting or master ownership for recording, mixing or mastering work, unless we sign a separate written production agreement. Ownership of the delivered files passes to you once the project is paid in full.

Production work that adds new musical material is covered by a separate written agreement before work begins, which sets out credit and any royalty or publishing split.

I may list the credit (for example "Mixed by Iain Findlay") on Muso.ai and similar credit services once the record is publicly released. I won't share unreleased audio without your permission.

By sending files you confirm that you have the right to use everything in them, including samples, and you agree to cover any claims that arise from material you supplied.

5. Loyalty program

  • How to qualify. Complete 10 paid bookings. A direct booking counts automatically once it has been paid in full. Sessions booked with me through SoundBetter count once I add them to your history. Bookings on other marketplaces count at my discretion.
  • What you get. When your final qualifying booking is paid in full, a 30 day rate lock opens. During that window you can book up to 3 projects at 25% off the standard service rates.
  • Expiration. The window closes 30 days after it opens. Unused discounted projects expire at that point and cannot be extended, banked or carried into a later window. Each further 10 paid bookings opens a new window.
  • Project limit. The discount applies to a maximum of 3 projects per window. It covers service rates and does not apply to add ons such as Live Drum Recording.
  • Non transferable. Progress and discounts belong to the billing email used at checkout. They can't be transferred, sold or shared with another person, artist, label or company.
  • How it's matched. There is no public lookup. Your booking history is matched privately to your billing email when you check out, and any discount you qualify for applies automatically. You can ask me for a private link to your own card.
  • No cash value. The program is a discount, not a gift card or stored value. It has no cash value and can't be combined with any other offer.
  • Changes. I may change or end the program with thirty days' notice on this page. A window that is already open will be honored until it expires.

6. Privacy

I don't sell or share your information, and you can ask me to delete it at any time once your project is complete.
